  • 3Caption-tone sticky headers: section titles go sentence-case first-person muted (like bb's “Pinned/Threads” captions), sentence case, blur underlay for stickiness. All-caps slab headers retire.
  • 4Titles upright: spool titles weight 550, thread titles 450, no italics anywhere.
  • 5Order inside a section: unspooled threads float top, spool deck beneath; spool members start flush under the header (kept from 0.7).
  • 6Depth & guides: members and section threads indent with the stock depth step and carry the stock right-edge family guide hairline.
  • 7Counted pips: neutral fg-tinted pill by default; success/warning/destructive tints when working/needs-you/failed — same grammar for section and spool pips; spinner overlays the numeral.

Color grammar — everything a tone may ever produce
teal 4 violet 4 success WORKING warning NEEDS YOU 2 destructive FAILED 1 primary unread dot + pip 1 neutral DONE 12

A tone may appear in exactly four places: a section dot, a word chip, a counted pip, or an unread dot/pip. Row surfaces, headers, titles and icon rails never take tone.
